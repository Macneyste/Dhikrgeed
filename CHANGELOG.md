# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (not yet released)

### Af-Soomaali

- **Badhamada codka:** codka hoos = hal tiro, codka kor = ka noqo (dejinta, waa xiran yahay marka hore). Shaashadda tirinta oo keliya.
- **Gariir kala duwan:** tiro kasta, dikri dhammaaday, wareeg dhammaaday iyo ka noqosho.
- **Shaashaddu ma demayso** inta aad tirinayso.
- **Kaydka:** dhoofi oo soo celi fayl JSON ah; waad xaqiijinaysaa ka hor inta aan waxba la beddelin.
- **Cirka** wuxuu raacaa waqtiga maalinta: waaberi, maalin, galab iyo habeen (xiddigo).
- **Beertayda:** wareeg kasta oo dhammaada waa geed yar, bil-bil loo kala saaray.
- **Taariikhda Hijriga** (Umm al-Qura) bogga hore, ±2 maalmood hagaajin.
- **Xusuusinta Jimcaha** (ikhtiyaari): Khamiis Maqribka kadib ama Jimce subaxdii.
- **Qibladda:** jihada iyo compass.
- **Habka Ramadaan:** Suxuur iyo Afur, tobanka habeen ee ugu dambeeya, tiriyaha Taraawiixda.
- **Soo sheeg cilad:** email aad adigu akhrinayso ka hor intaadan dirin.
- Telefoonnada daciifka ah: geedka iyo dhibcaha si dhaqso ah ayay u shaqeeyaan, font yar, R8.

### English

- **Volume keys:** Volume Down counts, Volume Up undoes (setting, off by default). Counter screen only.
- **Distinct haptics** for a count, an item, a round and undo.
- **Screen stays on** while counting.
- **Backup:** export and restore a JSON file, with a summary and confirmation before anything changes.
- **Sky** follows the time of day: dawn, day, dusk and night (with stars).
- **My garden:** every completed round is a small tree, grouped by month.
- **Hijri date** (Umm al-Qura) on Home, with a ±2 day adjustment.
- **Friday reminder** (optional): Thursday after Maghrib or Friday morning.
- **Qibla:** bearing and compass.
- **Ramadan mode:** Suhoor and iftar, the last ten nights, a Taraawiih counter.
- **Report a problem:** an email draft you read before sending.
- Low-end phones: memoized tree and beads, subset Arabic font, R8 and resource shrinking.
