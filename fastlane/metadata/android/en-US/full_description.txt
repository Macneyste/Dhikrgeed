Dhikrgeed is a tasbih and adhkar counter in Somali.

• Tap anywhere to count; a tree grows as you count.
• Tasbih (33 · 33 · 34), after-prayer, morning and evening adhkar, and your own dhikr.
• Count with the volume buttons, distinct haptics, screen stays on while counting.
• Prayer times with reminders you choose, Hijri date, Qibla and Ramadan mode.
• My garden: every completed round becomes a small tree.
• Export and restore your own backup.

No internet, no account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.
Open source (MIT).
